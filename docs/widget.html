Ledger Widget

Your Workshop stats on the iPhone home screen, using the free Scriptable app.

  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store.
  2. Tap Copy script below.
  3. Open Scriptable, tap +, paste, and rename the script to Workshop Ledger (tap the title at the top). Tap Done.
  4. Tap the script once to preview the widget.
  5. On your home screen, press and hold an empty spot, tap Edit → Add Widget, choose Scriptable and a size (small, medium or large), then add it.
  6. Press and hold the new widget → Edit Widget → Script → Workshop Ledger. Leave "When Interacting" on "Open URL".
Copied. Now paste it into Scriptable.

Small shows your totals; medium adds your top 4 mods; large adds every mod and your best rankings. Tapping the widget opens the dashboard. iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule, usually every 10–20 minutes.
